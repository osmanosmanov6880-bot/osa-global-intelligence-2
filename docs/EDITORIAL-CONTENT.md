# Interactive trilogy — editorial guide

## Series arc

- Part I — capability: AI is powerful pattern-learning software, not magic and not uniformly reliable.
- Part II — power: outcomes are shaped by data access, compute, deployment rules, accountability and who can contest decisions.
- Part III — agency: people can use AI to accelerate drafts and exploration while retaining responsibility for goals, verification and consequences.

## Interactions that are implemented

- The opening scene offers a human-first or AI-first strategy; the selected answer displays tailored feedback.
- The governance scene contrasts rapid deployment with auditability and appeal; the selected answer displays the corresponding trade-off.
- The final strategy scene contrasts observe-first, delegate-most, and human-AI partnership approaches; feedback changes based on the choice.
- Navigation between scenes and chapters is functional. Progress and choices persist in localStorage on the same browser/device.

## Content boundaries

The branching examples are scenarios created for learning, not factual case studies. No real individual or organisation is accused of conduct. The experience does not claim to include finished video, recorded narration, generated soundtrack, or server-side user progress. The current interface uses CSS motion and typographic staging; visual geometry is an abstract metaphor, not documentary imagery.

## Completion standard for future production

1. Replace the temporary typography-only title treatment with licensed/generative scene art only where it materially improves comprehension.
2. Commission or generate actual voice-over and soundtrack, then create accurate WebVTT from the final recording timeline.
3. Validate all quantitative claims against the exact primary source and edition.
4. Add server-side progress only after authentication and privacy requirements are decided.
