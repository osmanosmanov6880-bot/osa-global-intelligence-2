# Fact-check source register

This course intentionally avoids fabricated performance figures and claims that AI systems understand the world exactly as humans do. The interactive scenarios are educational thought experiments, not reports of real incidents.

## Primary and authoritative references

1. **Stanford HAI — AI Index Report**: https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index
   Use the specific edition and table when adding quantitative claims. Do not reuse older numbers as current 2026 figures without checking the report's publication date and methodology.
2. **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
   Reference for governance, validity, reliability, transparency, accountability and risk management.
3. **OECD AI Principles**: https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
   Reference for human-centred values, transparency, robustness and accountability.
4. **National Institute of Standards and Technology — Generative AI Profile**: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/generative-artificial-intelligence-profile
   Reference for generative-AI risks and evaluation practices.
5. **International Labour Organization — Generative AI and Jobs**: https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-refined-global-index-occupational-exposure
   Reference for task exposure; exposure is not the same as a job being eliminated.
6. **International Monetary Fund — AI and the Future of Work**: https://www.imf.org/en/Blogs/Articles/2024/01/14/ai-will-transform-the-global-economy-lets-make-sure-it-benefits-humanity
   Macro-level analysis; distinguish scenario analysis from forecasts.
7. **arXiv API documentation**: https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/
   Use primary research metadata and link to the original paper. Preprints are not automatically peer reviewed.
8. **Crossref REST API documentation**: https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/
   Bibliographic metadata only; a DOI record does not by itself validate a paper's claims.

## Editorial rule

For every numeric or time-sensitive claim, record: exact claim, source URL, publication date, access date, relevant section/table, caveat, and whether the claim is fact, interpretation, or projection. If the exact 2026 source cannot be checked, omit the number or label it as unverified. Never describe generated scene art as documentary footage.
