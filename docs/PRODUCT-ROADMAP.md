# OSA product roadmap — evidence before complexity

## Immediate: launchable learning experience

- Validate the four-transfer repository with the CI workflow.
- Run the interactive course on a real phone and desktop browser.
- Replace placeholder domain metadata with the actual verified domain.
- Add one real user test: can a first-time visitor complete the three chapters and describe the central insight?

## Next: course quality

- Produce real voice-over and licensed/generative scene assets in a separate media pipeline.
- Time subtitles from the final recording, not from estimated scene durations.
- Add bibliography links beside relevant claims and update them on a scheduled editorial review.
- Add chapter-end reflection and exportable completion summary.

## Then: persistence and revenue validation

- Only after user demand: add authenticated progress with a privacy policy and deletion flow.
- Test a paid pilot manually before implementing subscription billing.
- For payments, enforce entitlements server-side and verify signed webhooks; never rely on client state to grant paid access.
- Measure course completion, return visits, qualified email signups and paid conversion; do not invent metrics.

## Not yet

Do not build a complex recommendation engine, social network, or subscription tiers before repeat usage and willingness to pay are demonstrated. Do not promise guaranteed job outcomes or universal AI capability.
